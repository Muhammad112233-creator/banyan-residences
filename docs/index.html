At one of the Garden District’s most dynamic addresses, on Riverside Boulevard, Banyan is taking shape as a contemporary residential and commercial development that brings together refined architecture, everyday comfort and enduring value.

Location

What's around

Central Station is the district’s principal rail hub, served by the city loop and regional lines. Two platforms connect the Riverside Quarter to the old town, the business park and the wider metropolitan network in minutes.

The Meridian School is part of an education system unique in the region, with over twenty years of experience. Built on the foundation of an international curriculum, its results are the best proof of its leadership at every level of education.

Harbour Quay is a modern residential and business quarter known for its luxury apartments, shopping arcade and a 150-metre landmark tower. Pedestrian streets, restaurants and recreational facilities sit within a well-connected part of the city.

Atrium Business Park is the largest commercial complex in the district. Modern office buildings host international companies, restaurants, cafés and shops, making it an important corporate and economic hub.

The Glasshouse Market is a modern food and social hub bringing together local and international cuisines in one vibrant space. Its contemporary design and lively atmosphere make it a popular destination for meetings and after-work gatherings.

The International Airport is the country’s busiest, the primary hub for the national carrier, connecting the city with destinations across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and North America. It has undergone major modernisation in recent years.
